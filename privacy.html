Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Librox does not require an account and does not use advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking. We do not run a server that receives your books, reading history, or notes. Some features use Apple or the catalog you choose, as described below.

Your books and reading data

Books you import, your reading positions, bookmarks, notes, shelves, ratings, and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is available and enabled, supported data syncs through your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. Section 9 Labs does not have access to the contents of your private iCloud database.

Network requests

When you search, browse, or download from a catalog, Librox contacts that catalog directly. This may include Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Open Library, Unglue.it, or an OPDS catalog you add. As with any internet request, the destination can receive network information such as your IP address. Catalog credentials you save are stored in the device Keychain and sent only to the relevant catalog. Cover and book metadata lookups may also contact their source directly.

This website

librox.app is a static site with no advertising cookies or analytics scripts. The book search on the Find books page sends your query directly from your browser to the selected catalog (Gutendex or Standard Ebooks). Your reading-theme choice applies only to your current visit. The web host may process routine request logs as part of delivering the site.

Text-to-speech

Read-aloud narration runs on your device; book text is not sent to us for speech generation. If you choose to download an enhanced voice model, the download contacts its model host. It does not upload your book text for narration.

App Store purchases

Librox Plus is coming soon and is not yet available to purchase. When available, purchases and subscription management will use Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy practices; Librox will use the purchase status needed to unlock Plus.
