Support

Something not working, or a book not rendering the way it should? We'd like to hear about it.

Email matias@section9labs.com and include:

  • your device and iOS version (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 26)
  • the Librox version shown in Settings (e.g. 1.1 (7))
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

If the problem is with a specific public-domain book, a link to the book helps a lot.
