# Librox

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web marketing site for a native Apple reading app.

## Stack

Existing static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Blender Cycles renders the active 3D product film;
Remotion retains the earlier static-bezel alternative. Static assets remain local.

## Product purpose

Show the Librox reading and listening experience, then provide a small, clear
App Store download. The site also explains the proposed Librox Plus plan and
provides Privacy, Terms and Support subpages.

## Confirmed direction

The user requests a hyperrealistic, animated app showcase comparable to the
TypeDraw iPhone Duo reference film. Real app screens belong inside the device.
Show opening, page turns, listening, iPad and macOS. Use Librox 2.0 colors and
book artwork. Keep the page compact; avoid long scroll-locked sequences.

## Constraints

- Public Plus copy focuses on features and directs readers to the app for options and pricing at launch. No live purchase is implemented.
- Use full real books with reference covers, never the excerpt fixture as hero.
- No physical Duo is available. Captures come from development simulators.
- The user authorized shipping the original 3D studio films on October 19,
  2026. They are original studies, not official Apple art or physical footage.
  Public pages omit internal preview labels at the user’s request.
- Existing Privacy, Terms, Support and platform-aware App Store links remain.
- Custom OPDS catalogs stay free. Plus proposes offline voices, queues and
  per-book listening preferences, as the existing Plus page specifies.

## Evidence

Native Librox code and simulator builds, native Metal opening studies, app
screenshots, officially licensed static Apple bezels, and the user's reference
film and sites. Figma layout reference exists; its connected account currently
has an MCP quota limit. No generated asset purchase is authorized.
