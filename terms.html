Librox Plus

Librox Plus is coming soon and is not yet available to purchase. At launch, the app will show the included benefits, supported devices, price in your local currency, renewal period, and cancellation and restore information before purchase.

The planned US prices are $0.99 per month, $9.99 per year, or $49.99 for Lifetime. All three options include the same Plus features. Prices in other currencies and available plans will be shown in the app before purchase. See what Free and Plus include.

When Plus is available, Apple will handle purchases through your Apple Account. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before renewal; cancellation retains access through the paid period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase for permanent Plus access and does not renew. Recurring subscriptions can be managed through your Apple Account, and purchases can be restored in Librox.

If Plus ends, your existing books, reading progress, notes and highlights remain accessible. Free limits apply to new book imports and custom catalog additions. Basic and System listening remain available, and your selected app icon stays in place.
