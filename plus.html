A few useful details.

What happens if my membership ends?

Your existing books, progress, notes and highlights stay with you. You can keep reading and using Basic or System listening. The free limits apply when you add books or custom catalogs; removing a book makes room for another. Your selected app icon stays in place.

Is Lifetime a subscription?

No. Lifetime is a single $49.99 purchase for permanent Plus access, with no recurring charge. Monthly and yearly plans include the same Plus features for their paid period.

How will I buy or manage Plus?

When Plus launches, purchase in Librox through the App Store. The app will show the price in your local currency, available plans, device requirements and terms before confirmation. You can restore purchases in Librox and manage or cancel recurring plans through your Apple Account. Cancellation keeps access until the paid period ends.
