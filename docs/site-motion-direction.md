# Librox motion direction · October 18–21, 2026

The reference set is [Townhouse](https://townhouse.dev/), [Armor](https://www.armor-bd.com/), [Mapa do Astro](https://www.mapadoastro.com.br/), [Orizon](https://orizon.one/), [Reel Motion](https://reelmotion.fit/), and [Morpho](https://morphowater.com/), alongside the earlier [3D interactive websites video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fwWGfWjwEl0). The common direction is an immersive sequence with a focal object, sparse typography, and a meaningful change in scene as the visitor scrolls. Librox uses its own recorded app motion, ocean cover artwork, and green, paper, and coral palette.

## Film sequence

1. **The opening.** The first pinned scene shows the actual native SceneKit/Metal book-opening study. It plays once inside Apple's official static iPhone 17 Pro bezel and offers replay and pause controls. It is labeled as a shortened development sequence.
2. **The reading space.** Scrolling changes the full-viewport setting from dark cloth to paper, changes the text, and begins a continuous simulator recording of a cover tap, the reader opening, and two page turns inside the same bezel. It is labeled as development-build footage.
3. **The Duo preview.** Three pinned, full-viewport chapters change the Librox 2.0 library, page, and listening screens within Apple's official static iPhone Duo bezel. The chapter selectors move the page to the matching copy. These are labeled design previews.
4. **The page and discovery.** Reading themes and catalog search remain live interactions; subsequent motion should illustrate actual app capabilities rather than invented screens.

## Visual and motion rules

- Preserve Librox's ocean cover artwork, warm paper, dark green cloth, and coral accent. The ocean art appears in the opening, recedes into the paper scene, and returns in the editorial interlude.
- Show the app's authored native book motion. Do not model or animate Apple hardware; the official device bezels stay static.
- Keep real app recordings separate and visibly labeled. Generated imagery may establish an environment or light treatment; it must not be presented as app footage.
- Pause app video offscreen. Reduced-motion visitors get a stable still state on load; explicit play and replay still work. Every autoplay scene has a manual pause control.
- Prefer one purposeful interaction per chapter over decorative motion everywhere. The hero scroll changes the physical setting; the Duo selectors align the screen and chapter copy.
