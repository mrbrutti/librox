# Librox studio direction

The route is pinned by the user's existing Librox 2.0 identity, repeated
feedback and TypeDraw reference. No random FORM seed or separate generated
quality-card artifact was used. This records the actual implementation brief.

## THESIS

Let the visitor feel the app through a physical product film, then give them
a clear route to the App Store and a short explanation of the proposed Plus plan.

## OWN-WORLD

A forest-green reading studio. Cream Newsreader typography, restrained coral,
Manrope controls, soft light, reflective metal, glass, paper and honest app imagery.
The screen always belongs to Librox; book artwork stays inside the app.

## STORY

An original folding-device study opens. Librox's native Metal opening becomes
a reading spread. The camera moves close to native curls, then pulls back to
the listening colors before the device closes. Below, an automatic showcase
cycles through iPad reading, the listening design and native Mac footage.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

One full-height studio film with live HTML type and quiet navigation. Three
chapter controls, pause and opt-in sound. Separate wide and portrait renders
preserve the composition. No scroll locking or long pinned sequence.

## FORM

A compact film, one feature stage, download/Plus, and legal footer. Consistent
forest backgrounds. Standard document navigation to Books, Plus, Privacy,
Terms and Support. Pause on background/offscreen, explicit playback under
reduced motion, strong focus indicators, local media and content-hashed assets.

## Accuracy boundaries

Original 3D study, not official Apple hardware. Simulator and Catalyst footage,
not physical-device evidence. Library/listening views remain labeled design
previews. Plus is proposed at US $9.99/year; custom catalogs remain free.
The earlier Figma board is not synchronized to this rendered redesign.

## Visual quality reference

[TypeDraw: I turned iPhone Duo into a real-life notebook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jdVAIoRcYqM).
The relevant mechanisms are changing shot scale, a coherent studio light,
physical opening/closing, close page detail and smooth transitions. The film
uses original geometry and the project's native captures, not the reference media.
