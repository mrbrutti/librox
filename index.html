Three hand-tuned themes — try them on this page.

Read

Typography that disappears

New York, Georgia, Palatino, and bundled OpenDyslexic, set on a page that keeps your place when text reflows. Page turns the way you like them — Cut, Glint, or Curl — by tap, swipe, keyboard, or Bluetooth page-turner.

Listen

A voice that follows the page

On-device read-aloud highlights the sentence being spoken and turns the pages for you, with Lock Screen controls, adjustable speed, and a sleep timer. Book text never leaves your device.

Pace

A silent metronome

The guided pacer sweeps through the text at 150–900 words per minute, and reading focus dims everything but the current sentence — your eye always knows where to land.

Habit

Streaks you can actually keep

A daily reading goal, a gentle streak, and Want to Read / Reading / Finished shelves — backed by Open Library search for books you don't own yet.

Library

Yours, everywhere

Import from Files, AirDrop, the Share sheet, or the one-tap Safari extension. Discover free, DRM-free books from Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Unglue.it, and any OPDS catalog you add.

Private

No accounts. No ads. No tracking.

Books, positions, bookmarks, and notes sync through your own iCloud, invisible to us. Librox collects no data — there is nothing to collect it with.

Discover

Borrow from the commons.

Search the same free, DRM-free catalogs built into the app. Found something? One tap sends it straight to Librox on your iPhone or iPad.

    Searches go directly from your browser to Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks — nothing passes through servers we run, because we don't run any. “Add to Librox” opens the app; if nothing happens, install Librox first or use the EPUB link.
