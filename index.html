Most reading apps treat a book as a file. In Librox it is a book again: it has a cover, it opens, its pages turn, and it has a place on a shelf.

Your books stand face out, as bound volumes, in the order you put them. One that arrives without a cover is bound in cloth and stamped with a linocut that suits its title.

Tap a cover and the book comes off the shelf, opens on its hinge and puts you on the page.

E-ink is a plain white that stays easy in a bright room. Paper is a warm cream. Dark is ivory on true black. Change it and the book keeps your place.

Cut changes the page in an instant. Glint slides it across under a moving light. Curl lifts it like paper. These are recordings of the app.

Mark it up.

Drag the highlighter across a line and it is marked: a stroke of marker, an underline, a ring round a name. Every mark is kept in the book’s Journal. Try it on this page.
