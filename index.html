LIBROX

A calm, paper-quiet book reader for iPhone and iPad.

Librox reads your EPUB and TXT books with hand-tuned themes, careful typography, read-aloud narration, a guided reading pacer, and private iCloud sync. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

  • Three reading themes: E-Ink, Paper, and true-black Dark
  • Listen: on-device read-aloud that follows along on the page
  • A guided pacer for building reading speed (150–900 wpm)
  • Free classics from Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, and any OPDS catalog
  • Bookmarks, notes, shelves, and reading streaks — synced through your own iCloud
